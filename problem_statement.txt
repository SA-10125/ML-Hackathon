PROBLEM STATEMENT
PathSnap is a GPS trajectory map-matching challenge. 
Your team must build a machine learning-based system that takes noisy GPS coordinates and 
identifies the most likely roads travelled. The solution should combine a trained model for 
identifying road candidates with an HMM/Viterbi-based approach to reconstruct the most likely route. 
Predictions must be exposed through a FastAPI server that integrates with the evaluator interface.
